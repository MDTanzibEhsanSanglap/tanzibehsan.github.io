Biography

I am a PhD researcher in computational engineering at the University of Glasgow and a member of the Glasgow Computational Engineering Centre. My research focuses on the development and validation of finite element models for complex coupled physical processes.

My current work brings together nonlinear contact mechanics, measured surface roughness, electrostatics, and time-dependent electrical response to study triboelectric energy harvesting devices.

I am interested in applying numerical methods and scientific computing to demanding engineering simulation and R&D problems.
